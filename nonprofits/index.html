Rochester Nonprofits & How to Get Involved

Organizations making a difference in Rochester. How to volunteer, donate, and support your community.

Updated: November 2026

Rochester has an incredibly strong nonprofit community. Whether you want to volunteer your time, donate to a cause, or just learn about the organizations working to make this city better, this guide has you covered. Here are the nonprofits and community organizations that make Rochester a better place.

Food & Hunger

★

Foodlink

Rochester's Regional Food Bank

The backbone of hunger relief in the Rochester region. Foodlink distributes millions of pounds of food annually through 500+ partner agencies. They also run nutrition education, culinary training, and community food programs. Always needs volunteers for food sorting and distribution.

Volunteer: foodlinkny.org | Focus: Food distribution, nutrition education, culinary training

★

Open Door Mission

Homeless Services & Food Programs

Provides meals, shelter, addiction recovery, and job training for Rochester's homeless and at-risk population. Their programs serve thousands annually. Meal service volunteers are always needed.

Volunteer: opendoormission.com | Focus: Meals, shelter, recovery programs

★

St. Joseph's Neighborhood Center

Free Healthcare & Social Services

Provides free healthcare, dental care, and social services to uninsured and underinsured Rochester residents. A lifeline for people who fall through the cracks of the healthcare system.

Volunteer: sjncrochester.org | Focus: Free healthcare, dental, social services

Youth & Education

★

Boys & Girls Clubs of Rochester

Youth Development

After-school and summer programs for Rochester youth. Academic support, sports, arts, leadership development. Multiple locations across the city. Mentors and volunteers make a huge impact here.

Volunteer: bgcrochester.org | Focus: Youth programs, mentoring, education

★

YMCA of Greater Rochester

Community Health & Youth Services

Much more than a gym. The Y runs childcare, youth development, swim lessons, community health programs, and affordable fitness across multiple Rochester locations. Scholarship programs ensure access for everyone.

Volunteer: rochesterymca.org | Focus: Youth development, health, community programs

★

ROC the Future

Education Equity

A community-wide partnership focused on improving educational outcomes for Rochester's children from cradle to career. Data-driven approach to closing achievement gaps.

Learn more: rocthefuture.org | Focus: Education equity, youth outcomes

★

Center for Youth Services

At-Risk Youth Support

Crisis intervention, street outreach, residential programs, and counseling for at-risk and homeless youth in Rochester. They're on the front lines every day helping young people in crisis.

Volunteer: centerforyouth.net | Focus: Crisis services, outreach, youth shelter

Stay connected to Rochester's community.

Events, volunteer opportunities, and local news. Free, 3x/week.

Arts & Culture

★

Geva Theatre Center

Regional Theater

The most attended regional theater in New York State. Professional productions, community programs, and education initiatives. Their productions of A Christmas Carol and other shows are Rochester traditions.

Support: gevatheatre.org | Focus: Professional theater, education, community

★

Memorial Art Gallery (MAG)

Art Museum

University of Rochester's art museum with 12,000+ works spanning 5,000 years. Community programs, art classes, and the annual Clothesline Festival. Working toward "Free for All Forever" access starting 2027.

Support: mag.rochester.edu | Focus: Art exhibitions, education, community access

★

Rochester Contemporary Art Center (RoCo)

Contemporary Art Gallery

Free contemporary art exhibitions featuring local, national, and international artists. Community programs, artist residencies, and the popular 6x6 fundraiser where you can buy art for $20.

Volunteer: rochestercontemporary.org | Focus: Contemporary art, community engagement

Community & Social Services

★

United Way of Greater Rochester

Community Investment

Funds and supports 100+ nonprofit programs across the Rochester region. Focuses on education, financial stability, and health. The 2-1-1 helpline connects people to resources 24/7.

Get involved: unitedwayrocfingerlakes.org | Focus: Community funding, 2-1-1 helpline

★

Ibero-American Action League

Latino Community Services

The largest Latino-serving organization in Rochester. Education, job training, immigration services, healthcare navigation, and community programs serving Rochester's growing Latino population.

Support: ibero.org | Focus: Latino community services, education, job training

★

PathStone

Economic Development & Housing

Helps low-income families and communities achieve economic self-sufficiency through housing, workforce development, and community programs across the Rochester region and beyond.

Learn more: pathstone.org | Focus: Housing, workforce development, economic empowerment

Environment & Animals

★

Seneca Park Zoo Society

Wildlife Conservation

Supports Seneca Park Zoo's conservation programs, animal care, and education. The zoo focuses on species survival and connecting Rochester's community to wildlife. Great volunteer opportunities working with animals and education programs.

Volunteer: senecaparkzoo.org | Focus: Wildlife conservation, education

★

Lollypop Farm (Humane Society of Greater Rochester)

Animal Shelter & Rescue

Rochester's largest animal shelter and rescue. Thousands of animals adopted annually. Dog walking, cat socializing, and event volunteers are always needed. They also run cruelty investigations and community education.

Volunteer: lollypop.org | Focus: Animal rescue, adoption, humane education

★

Genesee Land Trust

Land Conservation

Protects natural areas, farmland, and scenic landscapes across the Genesee River region. They've conserved thousands of acres and maintain public trails. Trail maintenance and conservation volunteers welcome.

Get involved: geneseelandtrust.org | Focus: Land conservation, trails, farmland protection

Health

★

Golisano Children's Hospital Foundation

Pediatric Healthcare

Supports Golisano Children's Hospital at the University of Rochester Medical Center. Funds pediatric research, programs, and equipment for Rochester's children's hospital. Fundraising events and volunteer opportunities throughout the year.

Support: golisanochildrenshospital.org | Focus: Pediatric healthcare, research

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I volunteer in Rochester NY?

Top volunteer opportunities in Rochester include Foodlink (food sorting and distribution), Lollypop Farm (dog walking, cat socializing), Open Door Mission (meal service), Boys & Girls Clubs (youth mentoring), Seneca Park Zoo (education programs), and Genesee Land Trust (trail maintenance).

What are the biggest nonprofits in Rochester?

Major Rochester nonprofits include Foodlink (regional food bank), United Way of Greater Rochester, YMCA of Greater Rochester, Boys & Girls Clubs, Ibero-American Action League, PathStone, and Geva Theatre Center.

How can I donate to help Rochester's community?

You can donate directly to any of the organizations listed above through their websites. United Way of Greater Rochester also distributes funds to 100+ local programs, so a single donation there supports multiple causes.
