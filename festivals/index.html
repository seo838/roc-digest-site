Rochester Festivals & Events Calendar 2026

Every major festival, market, and annual event in Rochester, organized by season.

Updated: November 2026

Rochester is a festival city. From the Lilac Festival in spring to Roc Holiday Village in winter, there's always something going on. This is the complete guide to every major annual festival and event in Rochester NY, organized by season so you can plan ahead.

Spring (March - May)

Rochester Lilac Festival

May | Highland Park | Free

Rochester's signature event. 10 days, 500+ varieties of lilacs in bloom, live music, food vendors, craft beer tent, and 500,000+ visitors. The largest free festival of its kind in North America. If you do one Rochester festival, this is it. The park is stunning even on the non-festival days in May.

Flower City Days at the Market

May | Rochester Public Market | Free

The Public Market goes full flower mode in May. Hundreds of vendors with annuals, perennials, hanging baskets, herbs, and garden supplies. It's a Rochester spring tradition.

Summer (June - August)

CGI Rochester International Jazz Festival

June | Multiple Downtown Venues | Tickets vary

9 days, 1,500+ artists, 325+ shows across a dozen venues in downtown Rochester. One of the top jazz festivals in the world. Many free outdoor performances plus ticketed headliners at Kodak Hall, Kilbourn Hall, and other venues. The street energy during Jazz Fest is electric.

Party in the Park

Summer Thursdays | Manhattan Square Park | Free

Free outdoor concert series every Thursday evening in summer. Local and touring bands, food trucks, craft beer, and a great after-work vibe. One of the best free things in Rochester.

Corn Hill Arts Festival

July | Corn Hill neighborhood | Free

One of the top outdoor arts festivals in the country. 400+ artists, live music on multiple stages, food vendors, and the beautiful Corn Hill historic neighborhood as the backdrop. Two days of art, food, and community.

Park Ave Fest

August | Park Avenue | Free

Rochester's biggest street party. Park Ave shuts down for a massive block party with live music, food vendors, local businesses, art, and 300,000+ people. It's loud, crowded, and absolutely Rochester at its best. Usually the first Saturday in August.

Puerto Rican Festival

August | Frontier Field area | Varies

Rochester's vibrant Latino community celebrates with live music, traditional food, dance performances, and cultural exhibitions. One of the largest Puerto Rican festivals in the Northeast outside NYC.

Greek Festival

August | Church of the Annunciation | Varies

Three days of authentic Greek food (the lamb is legendary), live music, dancing, and culture. The gyros alone are worth the trip. One of Rochester's longest-running cultural festivals.

Fall (September - November)

KeyBank Rochester Fringe Festival

September | Multiple Downtown Venues | Many free shows

The largest fringe festival in the United States. 12 days, 500+ performances across downtown venues. Theater, comedy, music, dance, spoken word, and everything in between. Many shows are free. The atmosphere downtown during Fringe is incredible.

Clothesline Festival

September | Memorial Art Gallery | Admission fee

Juried art festival on the grounds of the Memorial Art Gallery. Artists display work on "clotheslines" across the lawn. Live music, food, and one of the most unique art festival formats anywhere.

Italian Festival

September/October | Various locations

Rochester's Italian heritage is strong, and this festival celebrates it with homemade pasta, cannoli, live music, and bocce. If you love Italian food, this is your weekend.

Winter (November - February)

Roc Holiday Village

Nov 27 - Dec 20 | MLK Jr. Memorial Park | Free

Rochester's signature winter event. 16 days of free ice skating, Santa visits, shopping village, fire pits, food vendors, and holiday celebrations. 2026 is the 8th annual. See our full Holiday Events guide.

Holidays at the Market

November/December | Rochester Public Market | Free

The Public Market's holiday tradition. Fresh-cut trees, wreaths, handmade gifts, specialty foods, horse-drawn carriage rides, and Santa visits. 25+ years of holiday magic. See dates.

Year-Round

Rochester Public Market

Every Saturday year-round + Tuesdays/Thursdays in season | Free

Not technically a festival, but it's Rochester's most beloved gathering place. 300+ vendors, global food options, fresh produce, flowers, local goods. Saturday mornings at the market are a Rochester institution. See our full market guide.

First Friday Gallery Nights

First Friday of every month | NOTA/Gallery District | Free

Art galleries across the Neighborhood of the Arts open their doors with free admission, artist receptions, and live music. It's a walking tour of Rochester's creative scene, and it happens every single month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the biggest festival in Rochester NY?

The Rochester Lilac Festival in May is the largest, drawing over 500,000 visitors over 10 days. Park Ave Fest in August draws 300,000+. The CGI Rochester International Jazz Festival in June attracts 200,000+ and is considered one of the top jazz festivals in the world.

What free festivals are in Rochester?

Many Rochester festivals are free including the Lilac Festival, Park Ave Fest, Corn Hill Arts Festival, Party in the Park summer concert series, Roc Holiday Village, First Friday gallery nights, and many Fringe Festival performances.
