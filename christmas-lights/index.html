Rochester Christmas Lights 2026: Every Light Show Worth the Drive

Drive-thru shows, walk-through displays, synced-to-music houses and day trips. Updated weekly through New Year's.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Looking for the best Christmas lights in Rochester, NY this year? We pulled together every holiday light show you can actually go to in 2026: the big ticketed drive-thrus, the free neighborhood displays synced to an FM station, indoor light shows for cold nights, and a few day trips that are worth the gas.

Dates and prices change, and some displays don't post hours until after Thanksgiving. Always check the official link before you load up the car. We update this page every week.

Quick Picks

  • Best drive-thru near Rochester: West Wind Farm Festival of Lights in Hilton (opens Dec 4)
  • Best free downtown night out: Roc Holiday Village + Liberty Pole Lighting (Dec 5)
  • Best for little kids on a cold night: Holiday Laser at the Strasenburgh Planetarium
  • Best self-guided tour: CAC Holiday Lights Tour (100+ decorated homes, benefits the Child Advocacy Center)
  • Biggest show within driving distance: Wegmans Lights on the Lake (Syracuse) or the Fairgrounds Festival of Lights (Hamburg)

Drive-Thru Light Shows

West Wind Farm Festival of Lights

Opens Fri, Dec 4 | Blue Barn Campus, Hilton | Timed tickets

The new home of the drive-thru show that used to run as ROC Lights at Victor Hills. Stay in your car and cruise the farm with the radio on. Carloads (1-7 people) are $40, Date Night (2 people) is $20, and the $50 Admission Plus adds four Pomona Express tickets with Santa photos, cocoa and crafts. Proceeds benefit C.U.R.E. Childhood Cancer. Buy timed tickets ahead; gate tickets are limited and cash only. Where: 928 Manitou Rd, Hilton. Tickets & details

Pete's Christmas Lights

Nightly through the season | Webster | FREE

A free drive-thru display synced to FM 97.3. Tip: enter from the start of the driveway, not where the Google pin drops you. Where: 1028 Bay Rd, Webster

Downtown & Walk-Through Lights

Roc Holiday Village

Nov 27 - Dec 20 | MLK Jr. Memorial Park, Downtown Rochester | FREE

Downtown turns into a lit-up winter village with free ice skating (Wegmans), Santa's Workshop, free kids' crafts in the Gingerbread House, fire pits and food. Opens Black Friday, 11 AM - 11 PM. Igloos, the magic show and story time with Santa are ticketed extras. Where: 353 Court St, Rochester. rocholidayvillage.com

Pittsford Candlelight Night

Tue, Dec 1, 5-8 PM | Village of Pittsford | FREE

The village lights up for the season with tree lightings at Main and State streets (5:30 PM) and the Schoen Place gazebo (6 PM), plus hayrides, live reindeer and carolers at Northfield Common. Shuttles run from Pittsford High School and Pittsford Plaza. Where: Village of Pittsford

Liberty Pole Lighting & Parade

Sat, Dec 5, 5 PM | Liberty Pole, Downtown | FREE

The official start of the season downtown. The Liberty Pole lights up at 5 PM with the Mayor, Santa and Prime Time Brass, then a parade heads to Roc Holiday Village at 5:15 with free Santa visits until 8 PM. Free parking at the Mortimer Street Garage. City of Rochester details

Lamberton Conservatory Holiday Show

Early Dec - early Jan (2026 dates TBA) | Highland Park | $2-4

A greenhouse full of poinsettias and Christmas lights, and it's warm inside. Last year it ran early December through mid-January with evening hours on select nights. Adults $4, youth and seniors $2, under 6 free. Where: 180 Reservoir Ave, Rochester. Monroe County Parks

Christmas at RCCM Musical Light Show

Dec 11-13 & Dec 16-23, 6-9 PM | Gates | FREE (register ahead)

Tens of thousands of color-changing lights animated across the RCCM campus, plus free indoor shows and a kids ornament workshop. Gates open at 6 PM nightly and each show runs about 20 minutes on a loop until 9. Tickets are free but you need to reserve them online at rccm.org. Where: 3177 Lyell Rd, Rochester

Indoor Light Shows (Great for Cold Nights)

Holiday Laser at Strasenburgh Planetarium

Nov 14 - Jan 3 | RMSC | $12-14

A 45-minute laser light show to holiday music under the dome. Adults $14, kids 3-18 and seniors $12. Shows after 6 PM are ages 5+. Book online to lock in a time. Where: 657 East Ave, Rochester. Showtimes

Neighborhood & House Displays

These are free, run by Rochester families, and most are synced to an FM station so you can watch from your car. Please be respectful: stay on the street, keep headlights low, and don't block driveways. Hours aren't always posted, so plan for roughly 5-10 PM.

CAC Holiday Lights Tour

Dec 5 - Jan 1 | Self-guided, all over Greater Rochester | $30 tour kit

The original Rochester lights tour. A $30 gift to the Bivona Child Advocacy Center gets you a tour kit with route maps to 100+ decorated homes and an activity book for the kids. Drive it as many times as you want. Get the tour kit

  • Golden Road Light Show (off Chestnut Ridge Rd): 42,000 lights synced to 20+ songs on 98.1 FM.
  • Orland Road Lights (East Irondequoit): 40,000 synced lights plus a seek-and-find for kids.
  • Bouckhart Avenue Walk-Through (Irondequoit): 28,000 lights, a 20-foot reindeer and a Santa mailbox on a walkable path.
  • Garford Road Light Show (East Irondequoit): Music on 106.9 FM. Santa night collects donations for Sunset House.
  • Celtic Lights (Greece): Bills-themed display with music on 90.9 FM.
  • Heatherwood Road (Fairport): Synced to 96.9 FM.
  • Lights on Santa Drive (Pittsford): About 12,800 lights on 103.3 FM, plus a backyard railroad.
  • ILLoominations (Burritt Rd, Hilton): Music on 99.1 FM with weekend character nights.
  • Raggi Christmas Light Show (Buckskin & Hunters Dr, Farmington): 20+ homes synced to 91.1 FM. Traditionally starts Thanksgiving night.
  • Rahway, Dawson & Winter Hazel (Gates): A whole neighborhood that goes all out. Some years there's hot cocoa on Winter Hazel Ct (bring cash).
  • ZLights (Rochester): Music-synced show, daily 6-10 PM, free.

Day Trips Worth the Drive

Yuletide in the Country at Genesee Country Village

Dec 4-6, 11-13, 18-19 | Mumford (about 30 min) | $35

Candle- and lantern-lit tours of seven decorated historic buildings. It feels like stepping into an old Christmas card. Tours are $35 ($30 members, under 4 free) and sell online only, with an optional dinner buffet sold separately. The Holiday Open House is Sun, Dec 20, 10 AM - 3 PM. Where: 1410 Flint Hill Rd, Mumford. gcv.org/yuletide

Wegmans Lights on the Lake

Nov 21 - Jan 4, 5-10 PM nightly | Onondaga Lake Park, Liverpool (about 1.5 hrs) | $10-20 per car

A two-mile drive-thru with 600,000+ lights. Mon-Thu is $10 per car and Fri-Sun is $20. Tickets are bought online for a specific night. Where: 106 Lake Dr, Liverpool. lightsonthelake.com

Fairgrounds Festival of Lights

Select nights Nov 27 - Dec 30 | Erie County Fairgrounds, Hamburg (about 1.25 hrs) | $22-45 per car

Western New York's biggest light show, with a drive-thru plus walk-around nights featuring Santa's Barnyard, a train ride and kiddie rides. Drive-thru-only nights start at $22 per car; full-experience nights run $34-45. Pick your night when you buy; popular dates sell out. Where: 5820 South Park Ave, Hamburg. fairgroundsholiday.com

Niagara Falls Winter Festival of Lights

Nov 14 - Jan 10, nightly | Niagara Falls, Ontario (about 1.5 hrs) | FREE

Canada's largest free outdoor light festival: 75+ displays, around 3 million lights, and fireworks on select nights. Bring your passport. Niagara Parks

Small-Town Lightings

Dec 4-5 | Finger Lakes & Genesee County
  • Avon Holiday Spectacular (Sat, Dec 5): tree lighting and mini light parade at 6 PM, free skating and a free family concert. Details
  • Penn Yan StarShine + Lights on Wheels Parade (Sat, Dec 5): lit-up tractors roll down Main St at 6:30 PM. Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the best drive-thru Christmas light show near Rochester?

The closest big drive-thru is the West Wind Farm Festival of Lights in Hilton, which opens December 4, 2026 ($40 per carload, timed tickets). For a bigger show, Wegmans Lights on the Lake in Liverpool and the Fairgrounds Festival of Lights in Hamburg are both under 1.5 hours away.

Are there free Christmas lights to see in Rochester?

Yes. Roc Holiday Village downtown is free to walk through, the Liberty Pole Lighting on December 5 is free, and dozens of neighborhood displays in Irondequoit, Greece, Gates, Pittsford, Fairport, Webster and Hilton are free to drive past. Pete's Christmas Lights in Webster is a free drive-thru.

When do Christmas light displays start in Rochester?

Most displays light up between Thanksgiving and the first weekend of December. Roc Holiday Village opens November 27, the CAC Holiday Lights Tour starts December 5, and West Wind Farm opens December 4. Most run through New Year's.

What is the Rochester holiday lights tour?

The CAC Holiday Lights Tour is a self-guided driving tour of 100+ decorated homes across Greater Rochester. A $30 gift to the Bivona Child Advocacy Center gets you the tour kit with route maps, and it runs December 5 through January 1.

What's a good indoor holiday light show in Rochester for kids?

Holiday Laser at the Strasenburgh Planetarium (RMSC, 657 East Ave) runs through January 3 and is $12-14. The Lamberton Conservatory holiday show in Highland Park is another warm, cheap option at $2-4.
