Best Restaurants in Rochester NY: A Local's Guide

35+ restaurants organized by cuisine. Updated for 2026.

Last updated: November 2026

Rochester's food scene doesn't get the national attention it deserves, but anyone who's eaten their way through the city knows the truth: the best restaurants in Rochester NY can hold their own against any mid-size city in the country. From James Beard semifinalists to no-frills taco joints, Chicken French to craft cocktail bars, there's a depth here that surprises even longtime locals.

Here are the best restaurants in Rochester, organized by what you're in the mood for.

Italian & Pasta

Pane Vino on the River

Downtown | $$-$$$ | Italian

Waterfront setting on the Genesee River with classic Italian. The tree-topped courtyard is gorgeous. Order this: Blackened green beans appetizer, then any of the handmade pastas.

Proietti's Italian Restaurant

Gates | $$ | Italian-American

Old-school Italian-American done right. Enormous portions, textbook Chicken French. Order this: Chicken French (a Rochester original) or veal Parmigiana.

Agatina's

Penfield | $$ | Italian

Family-run gem. Specials board is where the magic happens. Order this: Whatever's on the specials board, plus the antipasto platter.

New American & Fine Dining

Good Luck

Village Gate area | $$-$$$ | New American

Voted Best Restaurant in Rochester by CITY Magazine (2024 & 2025). The burger is legendary. Order this: The Good Luck Burger, truffle fries, and a seasonal cocktail.

REDD

Downtown | $$$-$$$$ | New American

2024 USA TODAY Restaurants of the Year. Rochester's best fine dining experience. Order this: The tasting menu if available.

Lento Restaurant

NOTA | $$-$$$ | New American

James Beard Best Chef Northeast semifinalist. Deep commitment to local and seasonal ingredients. Order this: Trust whatever's seasonal.

Chortke

Village Gate | $$$-$$$$ | Persian-American

One of Rochester's most exciting fine dining spots. Persian flavors fused with global influences. Order this: The date salad, wild mushroom, and any lamb dish.

Pizza

Pizza Wizard

Multiple locations | $ | Pizza

Best Pizza in Rochester (CITY Magazine 2025). Creative, well-executed pies. Order this: Whatever the special pie is that week.

Forno Tony

Fairport | $$ | Neapolitan Pizza

Serious Neapolitan-style from a custom wood-fired oven. Order this: The Margherita.

Strangebird

NOTA | $$-$$$ | Brewpub, Pizza

Best French Fries in Rochester two years running. Great pizza and house-brewed beers. Order this: The fries, a pizza, and a beer flight.

Mexican & Latin

Salena's Mexican Restaurant

Village Gate | $$ | Mexican

Rochester's favorite since 1998. Largest tequila selection in Upstate NY. Order this: Tequila lime chicken, street corn nachos, and a house margarita.

Bitter Honey

Rochester | $$ | Mexican

Lively taco bar with strong cocktails. Order this: Chimichanga and al pastor tacos.

Neno's

Monroe Avenue | $ | Mexican Street Food

Best Food Truck (CITY 2025), now brick-and-mortar. Order this: The tacos. Any of them.

Brunch

Voula's Greek Sweets

Monroe Avenue | $$ | Greek, Brunch

Best Brunch in Rochester 2025. Greek flavors meet American brunch classics. Order this: Any Greek-inspired brunch plate, plus baklava.

Jines

Park Avenue | $$ | American

Best Brunch 2024, Park Ave staple for decades. Order this: Classic eggs Benedict and a mimosa on the patio.

The Owl House

NOTA | $$ | Vegan-Friendly

Locally sourced, organic. Best Outdoor Dining (CITY Magazine). Order this: Chicken and Waffles or Buffalo Cauli Wings.

Asian

Poke Sushi

South Avenue | $$-$$$ | Sushi

Best Sushi in Rochester two years running. Fresh, well-executed. Order this: Omakase or specialty rolls.

Thali of India

South Winton Road | $$ | Indian

Best Indian Restaurant in Rochester 2024 & 2025. Order this: The lunch buffet.

BROTH on Main

Mercantile on Main | $$ | Vietnamese

Clean, deep flavors. Great pho. Order this: Pho and Vietnamese iced coffee.

Wings

Jeremiah's Tavern

Multiple locations | $-$$ | Wings

Best Wings in Rochester, back-to-back wins. Order this: Medium wings, extra crispy. Blue cheese.

Bar-Bill East

East Rochester | $$ | Wings, Pub

Buffalo-born wing institution's Rochester outpost. Order this: Medium wings and a beef on weck.

Vegetarian & Vegan

Red Fern

Oxford Street | $$ | Vegan

Best Vegan Menu Options in Rochester two years running. Order this: Portobello steak and mac and 'cheese.'

Neighborhood Gems

Dogtown

Monroe Avenue | $ | Hots, Comfort Food

Best spot to eat solo. Rochester-style hots, burgers, comfort food. Order this: A Texas hot with everything.

Cheesy Eddie's

South Avenue | $ | Bakery

Best Dessert in Rochester. Legendary cheesecake. Order this: Original cheesecake.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Rochester NY known for food?

Rochester is famous for the Garbage Plate, Chicken French (unique to the Rochester area), white hots, and an increasingly strong farm-to-table scene powered by Finger Lakes agriculture.

What is the best restaurant in Rochester NY?

Good Luck was voted Best Restaurant in Rochester by CITY Magazine for 2024 and 2025. REDD earned a spot on the 2024 USA TODAY Restaurants of the Year list.

Is Rochester NY a good food city?

Yes. The city has a James Beard semifinalist (Lento), a USA TODAY Restaurant of the Year (REDD), a thriving farm-to-table movement, and a range of independent restaurants across every cuisine type.

What are the best cheap eats in Rochester?

Dogtown (hots and burgers), Neno's (Mexican street food), Chuy's Taqueria (tacos), Seasons' Noodle (hand-pulled noodles), and BROTH on Main (Vietnamese pho). Great food under $15.
