Things to Do with Kids in Rochester NY

The local family guide. 30 activities from The Strong to Stokoe Farms.

Last updated: November 2026

If you're looking for things to do with kids in Rochester NY, you're living in one of the most family-friendly cities in the entire Northeast. This is a parent-to-parent guide from someone who has dragged strollers, packed snack bags, and survived the "I'm bored" chorus more times than I can count.

Museums & Learning

1

The Strong National Museum of Play

Downtown | Ages: All (best 2-12) | $22-23/person

Rochester's crown jewel. The only museum in the world dedicated entirely to play. 150,000 sq ft: mini Wegmans, butterfly garden, Sesame Street replica, National Toy Hall of Fame. Plan 3-4 hours. Tip: Get there at 9 AM. By 10:30 the lines are deep.

2

RMSC + Strasenburgh Planetarium

657 East Ave | Ages: 3+ (best 5-14) | $18-20

Three stories of hands-on science, planetarium laser shows, dinosaur fossils, and a water exhibit kids love. Tip: Check libraries for V.I.P. discount passes.

3

George Eastman Museum

900 East Ave | Ages: 5+ | $18 adults, $7 kids

World's oldest photography museum in Kodak founder's mansion. Discovery Room is great for kids -- make sun prints, animation strips. Tip: Head straight to the Discovery Room.

Outdoor Adventures

4

Seneca Park Zoo

2222 St. Paul St | All ages | $14-16

Compact, manageable zoo perfect for young families. Lions, elephants, penguins, red pandas. Done in 2-3 hours without meltdowns. Tip: Visit spring or fall for best animal activity.

5

Mendon Ponds Park

95 Douglas Rd, Honeoye Falls | All ages | Free

2,500 acres. Hiking, ponds, nature center. The chickadees will eat from your hand -- kids lose their minds. Tip: Bring birdseed for the Birdsong Trail.

6

Ontario Beach Park

4799 Lake Ave | All ages | Free

Sandy beach, pier, 1905 Dentzel carousel, and Abbott's frozen custard nearby. Classic Rochester family day. Tip: Get the chocolate almond at Abbott's.

Indoor Play & Entertainment

7

Bounce Hopper

Pittsford | Ages 2-12

NY State's largest indoor inflatable playground. Separate Kid Zone for toddlers. Parents can sit and breathe. Tip: America's tallest indoor inflatable slide.

8

Ontario Play & Cafe

Henrietta | Ages 1-8

Calm, screen-free option. Sensory play, imaginative play, and a cafe for parents. Tip: Best for the toddler-preschool crowd.

9

Altitude Trampoline Park

1399 Ridgeway Ave | Ages 3+

Wall-to-wall trampolines, foam pits, dodgeball courts, ninja warrior obstacles. Tip: Go weekday mornings to avoid crowds.

Sports & Active Fun

10

Rochester Americans Hockey

Blue Cross Arena | Ages 4+ | From $22

AHL hockey at a price that works for a family of four. The arena is small -- every seat has a good view. Tip: Friday/Saturday night games have the best atmosphere.

11

Rochester Red Wings Baseball

Innovative Field | All ages | From $12

Minor league baseball done right. $1 hot dog night Thursdays. Kids zone with inflatables. Fireworks nights. Tip: This IS the summer family activity in Rochester.

Seasonal Events

Roc Holiday Village (Nov-Dec)

MLK Jr. Park | All ages | Free

16 days of holiday festivities. Free ice skating with free rentals, Santa visits, crafts. 2026 dates: Nov 27 - Dec 20. Tip: Weekday evenings are calmer than opening day.

Rochester Lilac Festival (May)

Highland Park | All ages | Free

Rochester's biggest annual event. 500,000+ visitors over 10 days. Music, food, crafts, parade. Tip: Go on a weekday morning.

Stokoe Farms (Fall)

Scottsville | Ages 2-12

THE fall farm experience. Corn maze, hayrides, pumpkin patch, apple cider donuts. Tip: Go early on weekends before the rush.

Seabreeze Amusement Park (Summer)

4600 Culver Rd | Ages 3+

Rochester's own amusement park on Lake Ontario. Classic rides, water park, the historic Jack Rabbit coaster. Tip: Season pass pays for itself in 2-3 visits.

Rainy Day Ideas

  • Local libraries: Free story times, LEGO clubs, craft sessions at every Monroe County branch
  • Bowling: Radio Social (hip vibe + great food), Olympic Bowl (cosmic bowling), L&M Lanes (old-school)
  • RMSC Planetarium Laser Shows: Dark room + music + trippy visuals = kids are mesmerized. Holiday shows Nov-Dec.
  • Craft studios: Flower City Arts Center (real art instruction), Color Me Mine (pottery painting)

Day Trips (Within 30 Minutes)

Genesee Country Village & Museum

Mumford (25 min) | Ages 4+ | $20/$12

Largest living history museum in NY State. 68-building historic village with costumed interpreters. Kids do 19th-century activities.

Lollypop Farm

Fairport (15 min) | All ages | Free

Free admission to visit farm animals. Goats, pigs, horses, chickens. Low-key, no-pressure family outing.

Letchworth State Park

Castile (45 min) | All ages | $10 vehicle

"Grand Canyon of the East." 600-foot gorge, three waterfalls, stroller-accessible viewpoints. Pack a picnic.

Wild Wings Inc.

Honeoye Falls (20 min) | Ages 3+

Wildlife sanctuary with up-close encounters with owls, hawks, and eagles. Kids are mesmerized.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the #1 thing to do with kids in Rochester NY?

The Strong National Museum of Play. It's the only museum in the world dedicated entirely to play, with 150,000 square feet of interactive exhibits. Most families need 3-4 hours.

What are the best free things to do with kids in Rochester?

Mendon Ponds Park (hand-feed chickadees), Lollypop Farm (free animal visits), Highland Park, Genesee Riverway Trail, Ontario Beach, library programs, and Roc Holiday Village free ice skating.

What can kids do in Rochester when it rains?

The Strong Museum, RMSC, Bounce Hopper, Ontario Play & Cafe, Altitude Trampoline Park, bowling, Clubhouse Fun Center, and library programs.

Is Rochester NY a good place to raise a family?

Yes. Affordable housing, excellent parks, top-rated museums, good suburban schools, and strong community feel. Four seasons provide year-round activities from summer festivals to winter ice skating.
