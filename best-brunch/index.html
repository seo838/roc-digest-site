Best Brunch in Rochester NY

Where to go for pancakes, mimosas, eggs benedict, and everything brunch. Ranked by locals.

Updated: November 2026

Rochester's brunch scene has quietly become one of the best in upstate New York. From classic diners that have been flipping pancakes for decades to trendy spots where the avocado toast comes with a cocktail menu, there's a brunch spot for every mood and budget. Here are the best brunch spots in Rochester, according to people who actually go every weekend.

The Top Tier

1

Jines Restaurant

Park Ave | $$ | Weekend brunch all day

The most iconic brunch spot in Rochester. The patio on Park Ave in warm weather is unbeatable. Inside or out, the menu covers everything from French toast to benedicts to crepes. It's always busy and there's usually a wait -- and it's always worth it.

Order this: Eggs Benedict or the French toast. Mimosa flight if they have it.

2

Highland Park Diner

Highland Park | $ | Open daily

A real diner. Chrome exterior, vinyl booths, the whole thing. The breakfast is classic American diner done right -- big portions, strong coffee, friendly service. This is where you go when you want breakfast, not "brunch."

Order this: Short stack of pancakes with bacon. Keep it simple.

3

Magnolia's

Neighborhood of the Arts | $$ | Weekends

Southern-inspired brunch in a cozy setting. The biscuits alone are worth the trip. Magnolia's does comfort food brunch better than almost anyone in the city -- think shrimp and grits, chicken and waffles, and biscuit sandwiches.

Order this: Chicken and waffles or the biscuit sandwich.

4

Owl House

Monroe Ave | $$ | Weekend brunch

Vegetarian/vegan-focused brunch that even meat-eaters love. Creative, flavorful, and the cocktail menu is strong. The vibe is chill and artsy. If you think plant-based brunch can't be great, Owl House will change your mind.

Order this: Tofu scramble or the vegan benedict. Trust the process.

Upscale & Trendy

5

TRATA

Park Ave | $$$ | Weekend brunch

Upscale brunch with a killer cocktail program. The space is beautiful, the food is elevated but not pretentious, and the bottomless mimosa situation makes it a popular celebration spot.

Order this: Lobster benedict or the brioche French toast.

6

Redd

East End | $$$ | Weekend brunch

Farm-to-table brunch with seasonal ingredients. Everything feels intentional and well-crafted. Great for a special occasion brunch or when you want to treat yourself.

Order this: Whatever seasonal special they're running. The pastry basket is always excellent.

7

Good Luck

Anderson Ave | $$$ | Weekend brunch

One of Rochester's best restaurants also does brunch, and it's exactly as good as you'd expect. Small menu, perfectly executed. The kind of brunch where every dish could be "the thing you order every time."

Order this: Shakshuka or the burger (yes, at brunch).

Casual & Neighborhood

8

Nosh

East Ave | $$ | Daily breakfast/brunch

Bright, friendly, and consistently good. Nosh does the basics really well -- great eggs, solid pancakes, good coffee. The kind of place that becomes your regular Saturday morning spot.

Order this: Build your own omelet or the breakfast burrito.

9

Swan Dive

South Wedge | $$ | Weekend brunch

Funky South Wedge spot with creative brunch dishes. The cocktails are inventive, the vibe is relaxed, and the food is a step above standard brunch fare without being fussy.

Order this: Brunch burger or the chilaquiles.

10

Lento

East End | $$ | Weekend brunch

Local, seasonal, and consistently great. Lento's brunch menu changes with the seasons but always features locally-sourced ingredients and creative preparations that set it apart from standard brunch menus.

Order this: Seasonal frittata or the cornmeal pancakes.

More Great Brunch Spots

  • Blu Wolf Bistro (Victor) -- Worth the suburban drive. Upscale bistro brunch with great mimosas.
  • The Cub Room (South Wedge) -- Small, intimate, the kind of brunch spot that feels like a secret.
  • Daily Refresher (Neighborhood of the Arts) -- Craft cocktails meet creative brunch. Great for a boozy late morning.
  • Atlas Eats (Monroe Ave) -- Globally-inspired brunch dishes. Try the shakshuka.
  • Cure (Neighborhood of the Arts) -- Gastropub brunch with generous portions and a great beer list.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best brunch restaurant in Rochester NY?

Jines Restaurant on Park Ave is widely considered the best brunch in Rochester. The patio seating, extensive menu, and consistent quality make it a local favorite. For upscale brunch, TRATA and Redd are top picks. For casual comfort, Highland Park Diner and Magnolia's are excellent.

Where can I get vegan brunch in Rochester?

Owl House on Monroe Ave is the go-to for vegan and vegetarian brunch in Rochester. Their tofu scramble and vegan benedicts are favorites even among non-vegans. Red Fern also offers fully plant-based options.

What Rochester restaurants serve bottomless mimosas?

TRATA on Park Ave and several other upscale brunch spots offer bottomless mimosa deals on weekends. Check specific restaurants for current availability and pricing.

What time does brunch start in Rochester?

Most Rochester brunch spots open between 9-10 AM on weekends. Diners like Highland Park Diner open earlier (around 7 AM). Peak brunch hours are 10 AM - 1 PM on Saturday and Sunday, so expect waits at popular spots during this window.
