Rochester Holiday Events 2026: The Complete Guide

Every holiday event in Rochester from now through December. Updated weekly.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Looking for Rochester holiday events in 2026? This is the only guide you need. We've rounded up every major holiday event happening in Rochester and the surrounding area from now through the end of December -- festivals, markets, shows, free activities, and family events.

Bookmark this page. We update it every Monday as new events are announced.

The Big Ones

Roc Holiday Village

Nov 27 - Dec 20 | MLK Jr. Memorial Park, Downtown Rochester | FREE

Rochester's biggest holiday event returns for its 8th year, opening on Black Friday for the first time. 16 days of free ice skating and skate rentals (Wegmans), a Shopping Village, Santa's Workshop with free photo prints, the Gingerbread House with free kids crafts, fire pits, food trucks and free live music in the Triple-O Lodge.

Special Events at the Village:

  • Nov 27: Opening day, 11 AM - 11 PM, with free live music from The Isotopes (8 PM) and trivia night
  • Nov 28: Winter Wonderland Run 5K (4:15 PM, starts on Chestnut St) and Holiday Wreath Decorating (ticketed)
  • Nov 29: Kwanzaa celebration, 11 AM - 6 PM
  • Dec 5: Liberty Pole Lighting and Parade, arriving at the Village around 5:40 PM
  • Dec 6: Three Kings Day celebration
  • Dec 9 & 16: Mini Makers Market (4-9 PM) and Band Karaoke in the Lodge
  • Dec 10 & 17: Silent Disco on Ice (8-10 PM)
  • Dec 12: Figure skating performance + free learn-to-skate lesson (noon-1:30 PM)
  • Dec 13: Hanukkah celebration + Rochester Sled Hawks sled hockey demo (1-3 PM)
  • Dec 19: Curling demo (11 AM - 1 PM)
  • Dec 20: Christmas celebration on closing day

Open: Fri-Sun Nov 27-29 and Dec 4-6, then Wed-Sun Dec 9-13 and Dec 16-20. Fridays 4-11 PM (opening day starts at 11 AM), Saturdays 11 AM - 11 PM, Sundays 11 AM - 6 PM, Wed/Thu 4-10 PM. Shopping and Santa hours are shorter, so check rocholidayvillage.com.
Where: Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial Park, 353 Court St, Rochester

A Christmas Carol at Geva Theatre

Nov 25 - Dec 27 | Geva Theatre Center | Tickets required

Rochester's favorite holiday tradition returns on the Wilson Stage, in the version that begins with a wish from young Tim Cratchit. Over one night, Ebenezer Scrooge is visited by four ghosts. About two hours with intermission; recommended for ages 10+. Where: 75 Woodbury Blvd, Rochester

The Nutcracker with Rochester City Ballet

Nov 25, 27-29 | Kodak Hall at Eastman Theatre | Tickets required

The Rochester Philharmonic Orchestra performs Tchaikovsky's score live while Rochester City Ballet dances, for the 27th year. Six shows: Wed Nov 25 at 7 PM, Fri and Sat at 2 and 7 PM, Sun Nov 29 at 2 PM. Where: 26 Gibbs Street, Rochester

Holiday Markets & Shopping

Holidays at the Market

Sun Nov 29 & Sun Dec 6 (8 AM - 2 PM), Thu Dec 10 (6-9 PM) | Rochester Public Market | FREE

A Rochester tradition for over 25 years. Dozens of vendors selling fresh-cut trees, wreaths, handmade gifts, specialty foods, and seasonal treats. Free horse-drawn carriage rides at every event. Kids can visit Santa 9 AM - noon on the Sundays and 6-8 PM on Thursday night. Where: 280 N. Union St, Rochester

Holiday Wine & Craft Marketplace

Nov 27-28 | Casa Larga Vineyards

Wine tasting meets holiday shopping. Local artisans plus Casa Larga's award-winning wines. Great date night option. Where: 2287 Turk Hill Rd, Fairport

Native Made Market

Sat Nov 28, 10 AM - 5 PM | Central Library | FREE

The 5th annual Small Business Saturday market featuring Indigenous artists, entrepreneurs and educators from across the region. Family-friendly, free, and full of one-of-a-kind gifts. Where: 115 South Ave, Rochester

Nightmare Before Christmas: Immersive Shopping Experience

Nov 28-29, noon - 4:30 PM | ARTISANworks | $10

If your holiday aesthetic is more Jack Skellington than Santa Claus, this one's for you. Enter through "Halloween Town" or "Christmas Town" and shop 100+ vendors. Where: 565 Blossom Rd, Rochester

Park Ave Holiday Open House

Thu Dec 3, 5-8 PM | Park Avenue | FREE

The 33rd annual open house. Shops and restaurants roll out treats and specials, Mayor Evans kicks off the tree and menorah lighting, and local sports mascots and an RFD fire truck stop by. Where: Park Ave, Rochester

Holiday Shows & Performances

Holiday Laser at Strasenburgh Planetarium

Now through Jan 3 | Select dates | Ticketed

Underrated gem. Dark planetarium, holiday music from Mariah Carey to Mannheim Steamroller, and a new laser system. Works as a date night or family outing. Where: 657 East Ave, Rochester

Emmet Otter's Jugband Christmas

Nov 25 - Dec 12 | OFC Creations Theatre

Jim Henson's beloved holiday special comes to life on stage. Perfect for families. Where: OFC Creations Theatre, Rochester

The Sound of Music

Dec 1-6 | West Herr Auditorium Theatre | Tickets required

The touring Broadway production stops in Rochester for one week. A cozy family pick for early December. Where: 885 E Main St, Rochester

Cirque Musica Holiday Wonderland

Fri Dec 4, 7:30 PM | Kodak Center

Acrobatics meets holiday music. A circus-style holiday spectacular. Where: 200 W Ridge Rd, Rochester

Chris Thile & Aoife O'Donovan: Wassail!

Fri Dec 4, 7:30 PM | Kodak Hall at Eastman Theatre | Tickets required

Holiday songs, bluegrass style, from the mandolin great and the GRAMMY-winning singer. Where: 26 Gibbs St, Rochester

It's A Wonderful Life in Concert

Fri Dec 11, 7:30 PM | West Herr Auditorium Theatre | Tickets required

The RPO plays the score live while the 80th-anniversary film runs on the big screen. Where: 885 E Main St, Rochester

Let It Snow, A Holiday Cabaret

Dec 11-13 | Geva Theatre, Fielding Stage | Tickets required

Broadway veterans Chelsea and Geoff Packard come home for an intimate night of holiday classics. Where: 75 Woodbury Blvd, Rochester

Come From Away

Dec 11-13 | A Magical Journey Through Stages

Tony-winning musical about the small town that welcomed 7,000 stranded passengers after 9/11. About generosity and community. Where: West Herr Performing Arts Center, 875 E Main St

Jeff Tyzik's Gala Holiday Pops

Dec 18-19 | Kodak Hall at Eastman Theatre | Tickets required

The RPO's big holiday pops show. Friday at 7:30 PM, Saturday at 2 PM and 7:30 PM. Where: 26 Gibbs St, Rochester

Family & Kids Holiday Events

Christmas at RCCM

Dec 11-13, Dec 16-23 | Gates open 6 PM nightly | FREE (registration required)

Four free attractions plus a musical light show across the campus, running continuously 6-9 PM. Free tickets but register in advance because they go fast. Where: 3177 Lyell Rd, Gates

Stars with Santa at Strasenburgh Planetarium

Dec 5 | 9 AM

Kids meet Santa under the stars at the planetarium. Unique Rochester experience.

Holly Trolley Rides

Dec 6, 12, 13 | NY Museum of Transportation | $18 adults, $14 ages 3-14

A 2-mile round trip on a vintage trolley, plus Santa, hot chocolate and cookies. Reservations required. Where: 6393 East River Rd, West Henrietta

Seneca Park Zoo Holiday Magic Tours

Select dates Nov 27 - Dec 29 | Seneca Park Zoo

Guided walk through the zoo during the holiday season. Intimate, close-up experience. Where: 2222 St. Paul St, Rochester

Yuletide in the Country

Dec 4, 5, 11, 12, 18, 19 | Genesee Country Village & Museum

Theatrical tours and dinners in the historic village. GCV&M also hosts a Holiday Open House and Breakfast with St. Nick on Dec 20. Where: 1410 Flint Hill Rd, Mumford

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Roc Holiday Village open in 2026?

Roc Holiday Village opens Friday, November 27, 2026 (Black Friday) at 11 AM and runs on select days through Sunday, December 20, 2026 at Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial Park in downtown Rochester. It's open Fri-Sun the first two weekends, then Wed-Sun Dec 9-20. Admission is free, including ice skating and skate rentals.

What are the best free holiday events in Rochester?

The best free holiday events include Roc Holiday Village (free admission, free ice skating, free Santa visits), Holidays at the Market at Rochester Public Market, Christmas at RCCM (free with registration), Pittsford Candlelight Night, the Park Ave Holiday Open House, and community tree and menorah lightings throughout Monroe County.

Where can I see The Nutcracker in Rochester?

The Nutcracker is performed at Kodak Hall at Eastman Theatre with the Rochester Philharmonic Orchestra and Rochester City Ballet. Dates for 2026 are November 25, 27, 28, and 29. Tickets available at rpo.org.

What Christmas shows are playing in Rochester?

Major holiday shows include A Christmas Carol at Geva Theatre (Nov 25 - Dec 27), The Nutcracker at Eastman Theatre (Nov 25-29), The Sound of Music (Dec 1-6), Emmet Otter's Jugband Christmas at OFC Creations (through Dec 12), Cirque Musica Holiday Wonderland at Kodak Center (Dec 4), It's A Wonderful Life in Concert (Dec 11) and the RPO's Gala Holiday Pops (Dec 18-19).

Where can I meet Santa in Rochester?

Free Santa visits are available at Roc Holiday Village (Nov 27 - Dec 20), Holidays at the Market (Nov 29, Dec 6, Dec 10), Pittsford Candlelight Night (Dec 1) and the Liberty Pole Lighting (Dec 5), plus Breakfast with Santa events at the Lodge at Shadow Hill, Chili Community Center and Dave & Buster's in early December.
