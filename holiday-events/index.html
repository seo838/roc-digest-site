Rochester Holiday Events 2026: The Complete Guide

Every holiday event in Rochester from now through December. Updated weekly.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Looking for Rochester holiday events in 2026? This is the only guide you need. We've rounded up every major holiday event happening in Rochester and the surrounding area from now through the end of December -- festivals, markets, shows, free activities, and family events.

Bookmark this page. We update it every week as new events are announced.

The Big Ones

Roc Holiday Village

Nov 27 - Dec 20 | MLK Jr. Memorial Park, Downtown Rochester | FREE

Rochester's biggest holiday event returns for its 8th year. Opening on Black Friday for the first time. 16 days of holiday festivities including free ice skating and skate rentals (Wegmans), Shopping Village, Santa's Workshop with free photo prints, Gingerbread House with free kids crafts, fire pits, and food vendors.

Special Events at the Village:

  • Nov 29: Kwanzaa celebration (Gingerbread House)
  • Dec 5: Liberty Pole Lighting and Parade
  • Dec 6: Three Kings Day celebration
  • Dec 10: Silent Disco on the ice rink (8-10 PM)
  • Dec 12: Figure skating performance + free learn-to-skate clinic
  • Dec 13: Hanukkah celebration + sled hockey demo
  • Dec 17: Silent Disco on the ice rink (8-10 PM)
  • Dec 19: Curling demo by Rochester Curling Club
  • Dec 20: Christmas celebration + figure skating performance

Hours: Generally 11 AM - 10 PM on weekends, 4 PM - 10 PM on weekdays.
Where: Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial Park, 353 Court St, Rochester

A Christmas Carol at Geva Theatre

Nov 25 - Dec 27 | Geva Theatre Center | Tickets required

Rochester's favorite holiday tradition returns with a NEW version. Over the course of a single night, Ebenezer Scrooge is visited by four ghosts in this tale of hope, humor, and heart. If you do one holiday show this season, this is it. Where: 75 Woodbury Blvd, Rochester

The Nutcracker with Rochester City Ballet

Nov 25, 27-29 | Kodak Hall at Eastman Theatre | Tickets required

The Rochester Philharmonic Orchestra performs Tchaikovsky's score live while Rochester City Ballet dances. Eastman Theatre is one of the most beautiful venues in the state. Where: 26 Gibbs Street, Rochester

Holiday Markets & Shopping

Holidays at the Market

Nov 29, Dec 6, Dec 10 | Rochester Public Market | FREE

A Rochester tradition for over 25 years. Dozens of vendors selling fresh-cut trees, wreaths, handmade gifts, specialty foods, and seasonal treats. Free horse-drawn carriage rides. Kids can visit Santa. Where: 280 N. Union St, Rochester

Holiday Bazaar at RMSC

Nov 20-22 | Rochester Museum & Science Center

Three days of holiday shopping with unique vendors, crafts, and gifts. Combine shopping with a museum visit. Where: 657 East Ave, Rochester

Holiday Wine & Craft Marketplace

Nov 27-28 | Casa Larga Vineyards

Wine tasting meets holiday shopping. Local artisans plus Casa Larga's award-winning wines. Great date night option. Where: 2287 Turk Hill Rd, Fairport

Nightmare Before Christmas: Immersive Shopping Experience

Nov 28 | ARTISANworks

If your holiday aesthetic is more Jack Skellington than Santa Claus, this one's for you. Where: 565 Blossom Rd, Rochester

Holiday Shows & Performances

Holiday Laser at Strasenburgh Planetarium

Starting Nov 14 | Every Fri & Sat through the holidays | $10-12

Underrated gem. Dark planetarium, holiday music, laser light show. Works as a date night or family outing. Where: 657 East Ave, Rochester

Emmet Otter's Jugband Christmas

Starting Nov 25 | OFC Creations Theatre

The beloved holiday special comes to life on stage. Perfect for families. Where: OFC Creations Theatre, Irondequoit

Cirque Musica Holiday Wonderland

Dec 4 | Kodak Center

Acrobatics meets holiday music. A circus-style holiday spectacular. Where: 200 W Ridge Rd, Rochester

Come From Away

Dec 11-13 | A Magical Journey Through Stages

Tony-winning musical about the small town that welcomed 7,000 stranded passengers after 9/11. About generosity and community. Where: West Herr Performing Arts Center, 875 E Main St

Family & Kids Holiday Events

Christmas at RCCM

Dec 11-13, Dec 16-23 | Gates open 6 PM nightly | FREE (registration required)

A truly magical drive-through and walk-through Christmas experience. Free tickets but register in advance because they sell out fast.

Stars with Santa at Strasenburgh Planetarium

Dec 5 | 9 AM

Kids meet Santa under the stars at the planetarium. Unique Rochester experience.

Holly Trolley Rides

Dec 6, 13 | NY Museum of Transportation

Ride a vintage trolley decked out in holiday lights. Unique Rochester experience.

Seneca Park Zoo Holiday Magic Tours

Starting Nov 27 | Seneca Park Zoo

90-minute guided walk through the zoo during the holiday season. Intimate, close-up experience. Where: 2222 St. Paul St, Rochester

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Roc Holiday Village open in 2026?

Roc Holiday Village opens Friday, November 27, 2026 (Black Friday) and runs through Saturday, December 20, 2026 at Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial Park in downtown Rochester. Admission is free, including ice skating and skate rentals.

What are the best free holiday events in Rochester?

The best free holiday events include Roc Holiday Village (free admission, free ice skating, free Santa visits), Holidays at the Market at Rochester Public Market, Christmas at RCCM (free with registration), community tree lightings throughout Monroe County, and Holly Trolley Rides at the NY Museum of Transportation.

Where can I see The Nutcracker in Rochester?

The Nutcracker is performed at Kodak Hall at Eastman Theatre with the Rochester Philharmonic Orchestra and Rochester City Ballet. Dates for 2026 are November 25, 27, 28, and 29. Tickets available at rpo.org.

What Christmas shows are playing in Rochester?

Major holiday shows include A Christmas Carol at Geva Theatre (Nov 25 - Dec 27), The Nutcracker at Eastman Theatre (Nov 25-29), Emmet Otter's Jugband Christmas at OFC Creations, Holiday Laser at Strasenburgh Planetarium, and Cirque Musica Holiday Wonderland at Kodak Center.

Where can I meet Santa in Rochester?

Free Santa visits are available at Roc Holiday Village (Nov 27 - Dec 20), Holidays at the Market (Nov 29, Dec 6, Dec 10), and various Breakfast with Santa events at the Lodge at Shadow Hill, Chili Community Center, and Ontario Play & Cafe throughout December.
