New Restaurants in Rochester NY (2026)

Every new opening and closing, updated weekly.

Updated: November 23, 2026

Rochester's food scene is always evolving. New restaurants, bars, cafes, and food trucks are opening across the city. Here's what's new.

Recently Opened (Fall 2026)

Rimal Coffee House

Opened Nov 2026 | Downtown Rochester | Yemeni coffee house

An upscale Yemeni coffee house run by two brothers and two cousins, all Thomas Jefferson High School grads. It's in the same building as Patron Saint and Unwine'd downtown.

Dakshin Indian Cuisine

Opened Nov 2026 | Former Cub Room space | South Indian

The second location for the Syracuse South Indian spot, open now for takeout and delivery. Full-service dining comes once its liquor license is approved.

Mai Sho Kupon

Opened Nov 10, 2026 | The Mercantile on Main | Japanese milk bread

Soft, pillowy Japanese milk bread with five new flavors every month. A fun stop when you're downtown at the Mercantile.

MOSU Asian BBQ & Hot Pot

Opened late Oct 2026 | 655 Jefferson Rd, Henrietta | All-you-can-eat

In the former Red Lobster. Pick Asian BBQ (grill it yourself) or hot pot, with two hours per seating and vegan, halal and gluten-friendly options. Open 3-10 PM weekdays and noon-10 PM weekends; happy hour 3-6 PM Mon-Fri. Reservations at eatmosu.com.

Crunchy Lab

Opened Oct 23, 2026 | 2260 Clifford Ave, Rochester | Takeout fried chicken

Louisiana-style fried chicken meets Dominican pica pollo, with sides like tostones and chofan. Takeout only, open 11 AM - 11 PM daily. Order at crunchylabsny.com.
