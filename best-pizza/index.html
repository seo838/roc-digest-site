Best Pizza in Rochester NY

The definitive local ranking. From classic Rochester-style to wood-fired artisan and late-night slices.

Updated: November 2026

Rochester takes its pizza seriously. This isn't New York City thin-crust territory and it isn't Chicago deep dish. Rochester has its own thing going on -- and once you've had it, you get it. Here are the best pizza spots in Rochester, ranked by locals who've actually eaten at all of them.

Classic Rochester Pizza

The spots that define what Rochester pizza means. Big slices, good cheese, usually family-owned for decades.

1

The Pizza Stop

Downtown | $

131 State St. The one everyone argues about. Thin crust, foldable New York-style slices at a counter that's been there forever. Best cheap slice in the city. Cash-friendly, no frills, exactly what pizza should be.

Order this: Plain cheese slice, hot out of the oven.

2

Pontillo's Pizzeria

Multiple locations | $

A Rochester institution with locations across the area. Pontillo's has been doing the classic Rochester pizza thing for decades. Consistent, reliable, the kind of place you've been going to since you were a kid.

Order this: Pepperoni with hot sauce on the side.

3

Salvatore's Old Fashioned Pizzeria

Multiple locations | $$

The pizza empire of Rochester. Multiple locations, solid consistency, and a menu that goes way beyond pizza. Good for families, groups, and people who can't decide what they want because someone will be happy.

Order this: Stuffed pizza or the white pizza.

4

Nino's Pizzeria

Rochester | $

Big, fluffy, no-nonsense pizza. Nino's doesn't try to be fancy and that's the point. Good dough, good sauce, generous toppings. The kind of pizza that makes you say "this is exactly what I wanted."

Order this: Large pepperoni. Classic.

5

Cam's New York Pizzeria

Lyell Ave | $

A neighborhood gem on Lyell Ave that punches way above its weight. Thin crust, great sauce, the kind of place tourists would never find but locals swear by.

Order this: Grandma slice if they have it.

Wood-Fired & Artisan Pizza

For when you want something a little more elevated. Blistered crusts, local ingredients, craft cocktails on the side.

6

Restaurant Fiorella

Rochester Public Market | $$

Neapolitan-style wood-fired pizza at the Public Market. Housemade pasta and local ingredients. The char on the crust is perfect. This is the pizza you bring out-of-towners to when you want to impress them.

Order this: Margherita. Let the oven do the talking.

7

Pizza Wizard

South Wedge | $$

760 S Clinton Ave. Creative, slightly punk-rock energy. The menu changes, the vibes are great, and the pizza is legitimately some of the best artisan pie in Rochester. Small space, big flavor.

Order this: Whatever the special is. Trust them.

8

Ardor Park Artisanal Pizza

Park Ave | $$

117 Park Ave. Cozy Park Ave spot with thoughtful, well-crafted pizzas. Clean interior, kind staff, great date night option. The dough is the star here.

Order this: Any of the seasonal specials.

9

Tony D's

Corn Hill | $$

288 Exchange Blvd. Coal-fired oven, river views, Italian wine list. Tony D's is where you go when pizza is the excuse but the experience is the real reason. Great for a date or a nice dinner out.

Order this: Margherita from the coal-fired oven, plus a cocktail.

10

Ferrari Pizza Bar

Chili | $$

3240 Chili Ave. Wood-fired with a massive menu of creative pies. Three locations now and growing. Good for groups because there's something for everyone. The Whites Pizza is legendary.

Order this: Whites Pizza or the Bonacci.

Brewery + Pizza Combos

11

Strangebird

Wadsworth Square | $$

Brewery with serious pizza. The buff chicken pizza gets mentioned constantly. Great beer selection, chill atmosphere, and pizza that could stand on its own even without the drinks.

Order this: Buffalo chicken pizza + a house IPA.

12

Vern's

Park Ave | $$$

More of a restaurant than a pizza joint, but their pizzas are incredible. The lump crab pizza is a Rochester legend. Higher price point but worth it for a special meal.

Order this: Lump crab pizza. Not negotiable.

Late Night & By-the-Slice

13

The Pizza Stop

Downtown | $

Yes, it's listed twice. Because at 1 AM after a night out, there's only one answer. The downtown location stays open late and the slices hit different when you need them most.

14

Purple Monster Pizza

Rochester | $

Open until midnight. Hidden gem with amazing prices. If you haven't tried it, you're sleeping on one of Rochester's best-kept pizza secrets.

Order this: Pepperoni slice. Simple and perfect.

15

Pudgie's Pizza

Goodman St | $

1753 N Goodman. Over 1,400 reviews and still going strong. The kind of neighborhood pizza place every street in Rochester wishes it had. Consistent, affordable, and always solid.

Order this: Large cheese with garlic knots.

Honorable Mentions

  • Create A Pizza (Monroe Ave) -- Build your own with high-quality ingredients. Great vegan options.
  • 2 Ton Tony's (Titus Ave) -- "Not a better pizza in Rochester" say the regulars. Worth the drive.
  • Pizza Nota (Anderson Ave) -- Small, excellent, the kind of place you tell close friends about.
  • Linda's New York Pizzeria -- Classic NY-style that locals consistently rate as one of the best.
  • Mark's Pizzeria (Multiple locations) -- The chain that actually delivers consistent quality across Western NY.
  • FAVO Pizza & Draft House (Pittsford) -- Upscale casual with great salads and draft beer alongside solid pizza.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Rochester known for in terms of pizza?

Rochester has a unique pizza culture that blends New York City thin-crust influence with its own local style. Rochester pizza tends to be slightly thicker than NYC slices, with a chewier crust and generous cheese. The city also has a strong wood-fired artisan scene and several legendary by-the-slice spots.

What is the best cheap pizza in Rochester?

The Pizza Stop downtown is widely considered the best cheap slice in Rochester. Other affordable options include Nino's, Cam's New York Pizzeria, Pontillo's, and Purple Monster Pizza. Most of these spots offer slices for under $4.

Where can I get late-night pizza in Rochester?

The Pizza Stop downtown stays open late and is the go-to for post-bar pizza. Purple Monster Pizza is open until midnight. Mark's Pizzeria locations also offer late delivery in many areas.

What is the best wood-fired pizza in Rochester?

Restaurant Fiorella at Rochester Public Market, Pizza Wizard in the South Wedge, Tony D's in Corn Hill, and Ferrari Pizza Bar are the top wood-fired options. For Neapolitan-style specifically, Fiorella is hard to beat.

Where should I take visitors for pizza in Rochester?

For impressing out-of-towners: Restaurant Fiorella (artisan + Public Market setting), Tony D's (river views + coal-fired), or Vern's (upscale dining with incredible pizza). For showing them "real Rochester": The Pizza Stop for a quick slice or Pontillo's for the full local experience.
