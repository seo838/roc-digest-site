Things to Do in Rochester This Weekend

Curated by locals, updated every Monday. Thanksgiving weekend, Nov 27-29, 2026.

Last updated: November 23, 2026 (updated every Monday)

Looking for things to do in Rochester this weekend? It's Thanksgiving weekend, and the holiday season officially kicks off downtown. Here's what's worth your time, curated by locals and updated every Monday.

This Weekend's Top Picks

1. Roc Holiday Village Opening Weekend

Fri Nov 27 & Sat Nov 28, 11 AM - 11 PM; Sun Nov 29, 11 AM - 6 PM | MLK Jr. Memorial Park | FREE Family

The 8th annual village opens on Black Friday for the first time. Free skating and skate rentals (Wegmans), free Santa photos with a printed copy, free kids crafts in the Gingerbread House, a shopping village and food trucks. Free live music: The Isotopes Friday at 8 PM and the Jacob Merrill Quartet Saturday at 8 PM. Sunday brings a free Kwanzaa celebration (11 AM - 6 PM). The Winter Wonderland Run 5K starts outside the village Saturday at 4:15 PM. Where: 353 Court St, Rochester

2. The Nutcracker with the RPO and Rochester City Ballet

Fri Nov 27 at 2 & 7 PM, Sat Nov 28 at 2 & 7 PM, Sun Nov 29 at 2 PM | Kodak Hall at Eastman Theatre | Tickets required

Year 27 of the Rochester tradition, with the orchestra playing Tchaikovsky live. The Sunday matinee is the last show of the run, so don't wait. Where: 26 Gibbs St, Rochester

3. Holidays at the Market

Sun Nov 29, 8 AM - 2 PM | Rochester Public Market | FREE Family

The first of this year's three Holidays at the Market days. Fresh-cut trees and wreaths, handmade gifts, specialty foods, free horse-drawn carriage rides, and Santa from 9 AM to noon. Where: 280 N. Union St, Rochester

4. Knighthawks Home Opener

Sat Nov 28, 7 PM | Blue Cross Arena | Tickets required

The Rochester Knighthawks open their seventh season against the Halifax Thunderbirds, debuting new teal and purple uniforms. Loud, fast and a great night out. Where: One War Memorial Square, Rochester

5. Amerks vs. Syracuse Crunch

Fri Nov 27, 7:05 PM | Blue Cross Arena | From about $21

Black Friday hockey against the I-90 rival. The Amerks' only home game this week, then they head to Toronto for two. Where: One War Memorial Square, Rochester

6. A Christmas Carol at Geva

Opens Wed Nov 25; Sat Nov 28 at 7:30 PM this weekend | Geva Theatre | Tickets required

Rochester's favorite holiday show is back on the Wilson Stage through Dec 27. Saturday night is the only performance this weekend. Where: 75 Woodbury Blvd, Rochester

7. Nightmare Before Christmas: An Immersive Shopping Experience

Sat Nov 28 & Sun Nov 29, noon - 4:30 PM | ARTISANworks | $10 entry

Pick your entrance, "Halloween Town" or "Christmas Town," then shop 100+ vendors spread through ARTISANworks. Entry includes the gallery. Where: 565 Blossom Rd, Rochester

Thanksgiving Day (Thu, Nov 26)

The Feast Thanksgiving Day Run/Walk

Thu Nov 26 | Blue Cross Arena | Registration fee

Downtown's big Thanksgiving run. Kids' half-mile fun run at 8:30 AM (free, ages 10 and under), 5-mile run at 9 AM, 2-mile run/walk at 9:20 AM. Registration is open through race morning. Where: Starts and ends at Blue Cross Arena

Wedge Waddle

Thu Nov 26, 10 AM | South Wedge | FREE

A laid-back 3-mile run, walk or waddle. Day-of registration opens at 9:30 AM. Bring a pair of socks to donate to St. Joseph's House of Hospitality. Where: Starts and ends at Abundance Cooperative Market, 571 South Ave

Webster Turkey Trot

Thu Nov 26, 10 AM | Webster Park | Registration fee

The 55th annual 4.4-mile race draws thousands. Online registration closes Wednesday at 5:45 PM. Expect limited access on Holt, Whiting and Lake Roads from 8:30 AM. Where: Parkview Lodge, 255 Holt Rd, Webster

Night before: The Nutcracker (7 PM) and A Christmas Carol (7:30 PM) both open Wednesday, Nov 25, and Casa Larga hosts Thanksgiving Eve with Northside Johnny. Heads up: The Strong Museum is closed on Thanksgiving Day.

Free This Weekend

Native Made Market

Sat Nov 28, 10 AM - 5 PM | Central Library | FREE

The 5th annual Small Business Saturday market of Indigenous artists, makers and educators from across the region. Great for one-of-a-kind gifts. Where: 115 South Ave, Rochester

Free Skating at Roc Holiday Village

Fri-Sun | FREE

Free skating and skate rentals all weekend (pauses during scheduled rink activities). Rentals can run short on busy days, so bring your own skates if you have them.

Rochester Public Market

Saturday 6 AM - 1 PM | FREE

The regular Saturday market is on, then it goes full holiday mode on Sunday. 280 N. Union St.

Track Friday

Fri Nov 27, 8 - 11 AM | Irondequoit High School track | FREE

Skip the doorbusters. Running with Kelly invites anyone to run or walk laps for as long as they like, with donations collected for the Sunset House hospice home. No fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is there to do in Rochester this weekend?

Thanksgiving weekend (Nov 27-29, 2026) brings the opening of Roc Holiday Village downtown, the final Nutcracker performances at Eastman Theatre, Holidays at the Market on Sunday, the Knighthawks home opener Saturday and Amerks hockey Friday night. Check this page every Monday for the updated weekend guide.

What free things can I do in Rochester this weekend?

Free options include Roc Holiday Village (free admission, skating and Santa photos), Holidays at the Market on Sunday, the Native Made Market at Central Library on Saturday, the regular Saturday Public Market, and the Wedge Waddle on Thanksgiving morning. See our full guide: Free Things to Do in Rochester.

What family-friendly events are happening in Rochester this weekend?

Roc Holiday Village has free kids crafts, skating and Santa all weekend. The Nutcracker has 2 PM matinees Friday through Sunday, Santa visits Holidays at the Market Sunday morning, and Seneca Park Zoo's Holiday Magic Tours start Nov 27. Check Things to Do with Kids in Rochester for the full list.

Is there a Thanksgiving Day run in Rochester?

Yes, three. The Feast Thanksgiving Day Run/Walk starts at Blue Cross Arena (kids at 8:30 AM, 5-mile at 9 AM, 2-mile at 9:20 AM), the free Wedge Waddle starts at 10 AM at Abundance Cooperative Market, and the Webster Turkey Trot starts at 10 AM in Webster Park.
