Things to Do in Rochester This Weekend

Curated by locals, updated every Thursday. Nov 20-22, 2026.

Last updated: November 16, 2026 (updated every Thursday)

Looking for things to do in Rochester this weekend? Here's what's worth your time -- curated by locals, updated every Thursday.

This Weekend's Top 5

1. Holiday Bazaar at Rochester Museum & Science Center

Nov 20-22 | RMSC | Family

Three days of holiday shopping with unique vendors, handmade gifts, and seasonal finds. Perfect for knocking out some holiday gift shopping early. The museum itself is worth the trip too. Where: 657 East Ave, Rochester

2. Gary Owen: No Hard Feelings Tour

Nov 19 (Thursday) | Kodak Center | Tickets from $45

One of the most popular touring comedians right now. If you catch this weekend-adjacent show, you won't regret it. Where: 200 W Ridge Rd, Rochester

3. TopDog/UnderDog at Geva Theatre

Through Nov 22 | Geva Theatre | Tickets required

Final weekend for this Pulitzer Prize-winning play. If you haven't seen it yet, this is your last shot. Where: 75 Woodbury Blvd, Rochester

4. World Oddities Expo

Rochester Riverside Convention Center

Traveling oddities festival with artists, vendors, and macabre curiosities. Check availability. Where: Rochester Riverside Convention Center

5. Rochester Americans Hockey

Check amerks.com for this week's schedule | From $22

Always a solid weekend option. Affordable tickets, great atmosphere, cold beer. Where: Blue Cross Arena, One War Memorial Square

Free This Weekend

Rochester Public Market

Saturday 6 AM - 1 PM | FREE

The best Saturday morning in Rochester. Fresh produce, baked goods, global food vendors. 280 N. Union St. Always free.

Gallery Exhibitions

FREE

Image City Photography Gallery (722 University Ave) and Rochester Contemporary Art Center both have free shows running.

Self-Guided Fairy Door Hunt

Dansville | Dawn to dusk | Family FREE

Free family scavenger hunt. Cute day trip.

Open Skate Sessions

Gene Polisseni Center | Check schedule

Public skating times available on select days.

Food & Drink This Weekend

Public Market Saturday: Get there early for the best breakfast. Our picks: the empanadas at the Latin food stand, the doughnuts at Hedonist Artisan Chocolates, and whatever's fresh at the flower vendors.

Date Night Idea: Skip the chain restaurants. Try a spot on Park Ave or East Ave you haven't been to. Rochester's food scene has quietly become one of the best in upstate NY. See our restaurant guide →

Family & Kids

  • Rochester Museum & Science Center -- Holiday Bazaar doubles as a family outing
  • The Strong National Museum of Play -- Always open weekends. If you have kids under 10, it's a must.
  • Seneca Park Zoo -- Open year-round. Fall is a great time to visit -- fewer crowds, animals are active.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is there to do in Rochester this weekend?

Rochester always has something going on. This weekend features the Holiday Bazaar at RMSC, Rochester Americans hockey, live comedy and music at venues like Kodak Center and The Carlson, free activities at the Rochester Public Market, and ongoing shows at Geva Theatre. Check this page every Thursday for the updated weekend guide.

What free things can I do in Rochester this weekend?

Free options this weekend include the Rochester Public Market (Saturday mornings), free gallery exhibitions at Image City and Rochester Contemporary Art Center, public skating sessions, and self-guided walking trails along the Genesee Riverway. See our full guide: Free Things to Do in Rochester.

What family-friendly events are happening in Rochester this weekend?

Family-friendly options include the Holiday Bazaar at RMSC, The Strong National Museum of Play, Seneca Park Zoo, and various free library events throughout Monroe County. Check Things to Do with Kids in Rochester for the full list.

Where should I eat in Rochester this weekend?

Rochester's food scene is thriving. For weekend brunch, try Park Ave or East Ave restaurants. For Saturday morning, hit the Rochester Public Market. For dinner, see our Best Restaurants in Rochester Guide.
