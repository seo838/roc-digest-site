Free Things to Do in Rochester NY

35+ activities that cost nothing. Parks, museums, markets, festivals, and more.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Looking for free things to do in Rochester NY? Rochester has an absurd amount of things you can do without spending a dime. We're talking world-class parks, free museum days, outdoor trails, art walks, public markets, and more. These are genuinely good ways to spend your time.

Outdoor & Parks

1

Highland Park

171 Reservoir Ave | Free

150 acres of rolling hills, walking paths, and 1,200+ lilac bushes. Home to the Rochester Lilac Festival every May (500,000+ visitors). The Lamberton Conservatory has tropical plants year-round.

2

Cobbs Hill Park

Culver Rd & Norris Dr | Free

Best views in the city from the reservoir loop. Rochester skyline panoramic. In winter, the most popular sledding spot in the county.

3

Genesee Riverway Trail

24 miles through Rochester | Free

National Recreation Trail following the Genesee River. Walk, run, or bike. Connects Genesee Valley Park, Seneca Park, Maplewood Park, High Falls, and Turning Point Park.

4

Turning Point Park

20 Boxart St | Free

Hidden gem with a floating boardwalk along the Genesee River through marshland. Flat, stroller-friendly. See herons, turtles, kayakers.

5

Ontario Beach Park

4799 Lake Ave (Charlotte) | Free

Rochester's lakefront. Sandy beach, pier into Lake Ontario, 1905 Dentzel carousel, and free Concerts by the Shore on summer Wednesdays.

6

Mendon Ponds Park

95 Douglas Rd, Honeoye Falls | Free

2,600 acres, 30 miles of trails. Famous for hand-feeding chickadees. Bring sunflower seeds!

Free Museum Days & Culture

7

RMSC Amazon Free Afternoons

657 East Ave | 1st Wednesday, noon-5 PM

Free admission to the entire Rochester Museum & Science Center on the first Wednesday of each month. Includes planetarium.

8

Memorial Art Gallery (MAG)

500 University Ave

5,000 years of art. Centennial Sculpture Park is always free. Going permanently free for everyone starting May 1, 2027.

9

High Falls & Pont de Rennes Bridge

60 Brown's Race | Free

96-foot waterfall in the middle of downtown Rochester. Walk the bridge for a head-on view. Free year-round.

10

Rochester Contemporary Art Center

137 East Ave | Always Free

Free contemporary art space with rotating exhibits. The '6x6' shows feature hundreds of small artworks.

Markets & Community

11

Rochester Public Market

280 N Union St | Tue/Thu/Sat | Free

300+ vendors. Fresh produce, baked goods, ethnic foods, flowers. The Saturday market is the biggest. Special events year-round.

12

Village Gate Square

274 N Goodman St | Free

Converted industrial complex with galleries, studios, restaurants. Free to explore.

13

Park Avenue Neighborhood Walk

Park Ave between Culver & Goodman | Free

Rochester's most walkable neighborhood. Independent shops, restaurants, great people-watching.

Free Festivals (Annual)

  • Rochester Lilac Festival (May): 10 days, 500,000+ visitors, free music, food, art. Highland Park.
  • Rochester International Jazz Festival (June): Free outdoor stages and street performances.
  • Corn Hill Arts Festival (July): 400+ artists, free admission.
  • Party in the Park (Summer Thursdays): Free outdoor concerts at MLK Jr. Park.
  • Roc Holiday Village (Nov-Dec): Free ice skating with free rentals, Santa visits, shopping.
  • Concerts by the Shore (Summer Wednesdays): Free live music at Ontario Beach.
  • First Friday Gallery Nights (Monthly): Free gallery openings throughout NOTA and East End.

Family & Kids (Free)

  • Seneca Park Zoo - Book & Beast: Free storytime + live animal visit (1st & 3rd Wednesdays)
  • Libraries across Monroe County: Free storytimes, LEGO clubs, craft days, teen events
  • Helmer Nature Center: Free woodland trails and seasonal family programs
  • Playground hopping: Carter Park, Cobbs Hill, Perinton Park, Webster Park, Greece Canal Park

Seasonal & Winter

  • Roc Holiday Village Ice Skating: Free skating with free rentals (Nov 27 - Dec 20, 2026)
  • Holidays at the Market: Free admission, Santa visits and horse-drawn carriage rides (Sun Nov 29, Sun Dec 6, Thu Dec 10)
  • Pittsford Candlelight Night: Tree lightings, hayrides and live reindeer in the village (Tue Dec 1, 5-8 PM)
  • Liberty Pole Lighting & Parade: Downtown kickoff with Santa and Prime Time Brass (Sat Dec 5, 5 PM)
  • Christmas at RCCM: Free shows and light display in Gates, free tickets required (Dec 11-13 & 16-23)
  • Cobbs Hill Sledding: Rochester's unofficial sledding capital after any decent snowfall
  • Maplewood Rose Garden: 250+ rose cultivars, free, blooms June-November
  • Mount Hope Cemetery: First municipal Victorian cemetery in America (1838). Susan B. Anthony and Frederick Douglass are buried here.
  • Cross-country skiing: Mendon Ponds, Durand Eastman, Erie Canal towpath (bring your own gear)

Frequently Asked Questions

What free things are there to do in Rochester NY?

Top free options include Highland Park (1,200 lilac bushes), the 24-mile Genesee Riverway Trail, Rochester Public Market (Tue/Thu/Sat), High Falls waterfall, free museum afternoons at RMSC, First Friday gallery nights, and free festivals like the Lilac Festival in May.

Are there free museum days in Rochester?

Yes. RMSC hosts Amazon Free Afternoons on the first Wednesday monthly. Memorial Art Gallery will become permanently free starting May 2027. George Eastman Museum is free for SNAP/EBT cardholders. Rochester Contemporary is always free.

What can you do in Rochester with kids for free?

Library programs (every branch), Book & Beast at Seneca Park Zoo, RMSC Amazon Free Afternoons, dozens of public playgrounds, Mendon Ponds Park chickadee feeding, and Roc Holiday Village free ice skating.

What are the best free outdoor activities?

Genesee Riverway Trail (24 miles), Turning Point Park boardwalk, Erie Canal towpath biking, Ontario Beach pier, Mendon Ponds chickadee feeding, and Cobbs Hill sunset views.
