About The ROC Digest

Rochester's free insider newsletter, built by someone who's lived here.

Why We Built This

Rochester has a lot going on. The problem isn't that there's nothing to do -- it's that by the time you hear about it, the event already happened, the restaurant already had a two-hour wait, and the festival was last weekend.

We built The ROC Digest because Rochester deserves a better way to stay in the loop. Not a tourism board website that hasn't been updated since last season. Not a Facebook group where you have to scroll past 50 posts to find something useful. Just a clean, curated guide to what's actually happening in the city this week.

What We Cover

Three times a week, we send you the stuff worth knowing about:

  • Events -- concerts, shows, festivals, markets, community gatherings
  • Food -- new restaurant openings, best-of guides, deals, hidden spots
  • Things to Do -- weekend picks, family activities, free stuff, date night ideas
  • Hidden Gems -- the Rochester spots most people walk past without knowing
  • Community -- nonprofits, volunteer opportunities, local businesses making a difference

We also maintain an ever-growing library of local guides on this website -- from the best restaurants to free things to do to family activities. All written by locals, updated regularly, and designed to actually help you make plans.

Who We Are

The ROC Digest is built by people who live in Rochester. Not a corporate media company running the same template in 50 cities. Not an AI scraping event listings from other websites. We actually go to these restaurants, attend these events, and walk these trails.

That matters because when we say a restaurant is worth the trip or an event is worth your Saturday, we're speaking from experience -- not from a press release.
